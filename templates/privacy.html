Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 | Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Privacy Guarantee: Spendly respects your financial privacy. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal financial data to third parties or advertisers.

1. Introduction

Spendly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and securing your personal financial information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use the Spendly expense tracking application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide you with secure personal expense tracking services:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and securely hashed password when you create an account.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Expense amounts, category tags, dates, and descriptions that you manually enter into Spendly.
  • System & Session Data: Technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and essential session cookies required for authentication.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized to operate and improve your personal experience:

  • To render personal financial dashboards, expense breakdowns, and budget reports.
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account access.
  • To provide customer support and notify you of important service updates.

4. Financial Data Confidentiality & Protection

We adhere to strict privacy practices common among leading financial applications:

  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, or share your financial records or personal information with data brokers or marketing firms.
  • Password Encryption: Passwords are encrypted using one-way cryptographic hashing algorithms (Werkzeug / PBKDF2). Plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Data Isolation: Your transaction records are isolated to your account and accessible only by you.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You retain full ownership and control over your financial data:

  • Access & Modify: You can view, edit, or remove individual expense entries at any time from your account dashboard.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to request full account deletion, which permanently purges your user profile and all associated expense records from our database.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Spendly uses essential session cookies solely for user login authentication and security state management. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

Spendly is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data privacy rights, please contact our Privacy Team at privacy@spendly.com.
